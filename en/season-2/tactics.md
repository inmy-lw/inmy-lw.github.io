# Different tactics that will be used during Season 2

<a href="/last_war_tactics.html" target="_blank">Animations of the different tactics</a>

_You need to create a new empty scenario to access the saved scenarios..._

## Defense with the missile destroyed (before launch)

The goal here is to destroy the enemy warlord's HQ before they are able to launch the missile.

This way, furnace defense will resemble a Season 1 military base defense.

Different steps:
1. Locate the enemy warlord
2. Teleport the 4 captains as close as possible to the enemy warlord
3. Teleport hunters and guards as close as possible to the captains
4. The 4 captains launch rallies toward the enemy warlord.
   - Hunters and guards join the rallies as quickly as possible with **T1**.
   - The captain is reinforced with **T2**.
5. Hunters and guards launch solo attacks with the remaining teams (T3 and T4 if available) while following the rallies.

## Defense when the missile has already been launched

When the missile hits, it creates a mud zone around the furnace. The goal is to occupy this mud as quickly as possible after it appears. The defense will then resemble a Season 1 military base defense.

Different steps:
1. Defensive positioning around the alliance furnace
2. 30 seconds before missile impact, emergency evacuation using a **Random Teleporter**
3. Return to positions using the inter-server teleporter
4. Reinforce the furnace with guards
5. Defend the furnace

## Attack after a successful missile launch

Launching the missile makes it possible to remove defending enemy HQs. It will also be necessary to occupy the mud as quickly as possible after the missile strike.

Different steps:
1. Deploy the missile
2. Launch the missile
   - Hunters take the opportunity to attack enemy HQs
3. Teleport around the enemy furnace
   - Guards on the front line around the furnace
   - Walls behind the guards
   - Hunters on the back line, ready to attack approaching enemies
4. Attack the enemy furnace

## Attack after failing to launch the missile

This attack will resemble a properly defended military base attack from Season 1. The rallies must pull out enemy reinforcements first, then solo attacks must hit the furnace before the enemies have time to reinforce it again.

Different steps:
1. Captains move as close as possible to enemy HQs
2. Guards move as close as possible to the captains
3. Captains launch rallies with **T1** on the enemy furnace
4. Hunters join the rallies with **T1**
5. Launch solo attacks with all other remaining teams, which must follow the rallies
