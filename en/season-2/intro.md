## Seasonal Buildings

New temporary buildings will appear (they will disappear after the season): new military bases for tank, aircraft, and missile.
These buildings will be available once the High-Heat Furnace has reached level 30, and will only use seasonal resources to be improved (**Titanium Alloy** and **Coal**).

They will increase the HP of the concerned heroes by 1% per level.

![new building](/assets/img/season-2/seasonal-buildings.webp)

## Blizzard

Snowstorms are extreme weather events specific to Season 2. When a snowstorm triggers, the temperature drops sharply, exposing players' bases to a risk of freezing if they fall below -20°.

You will need to activate the automatic ignition of the High-Heat Furnace during storms.
For the Alliance Furnace, an R4 or the R5 will have to turn it on just before the storm.

When a snowstorm is about to arrive, you are informed via a notification at the top left of your screen several hours beforehand, indicating the additional temperature drop.

Snowstorms last several hours: it is important to always keep coal in your inventory to be able to activate the High-Heat Furnace.
Check your consumption per minute turned on with overdrive activated and keep the equivalent of 4 hours.
