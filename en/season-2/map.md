# Map

{% include image.html url="/assets/img/season-2/map.webp" description="New Season 2 Map" %}

This map is composed of very rectangular plots, all of which can be captured:

- Dig sites (unnamed, marked '1' on the image).
  - They will allow you to earn more coal.
- Cities (named, marked '2' on the image)
    - City capture will only be available progressively:
        - Week 1
          - Level 1 Cities
          - Level 2 Cities
        - Week 2
          - Level 3 Cities
          - Level 4 Cities
        - Week 3
          - Level 5 Cities
          - Level 6 Cities
        - Week 4
          - Capture of the nuclear furnace

Outside temperature varies depending on the position on the map. The closer you are to the center, the lower the temperature.
